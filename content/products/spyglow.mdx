SpyGlow is a competitive-intelligence product I founded and built to turn
changes on public competitor websites into evidence-backed actions.

## The product problem

Website monitoring is easy to describe and difficult to make trustworthy.
Pages fail to load, consent screens appear, navigation changes and minor markup
differences can look like meaningful business events. Sending every difference
to an AI model produces noisy alerts and unnecessary cost.

## The engineering approach

I designed the monitoring path as a layered pipeline. Capture-quality checks
reject weak snapshots, suspicious results are rechecked, and deterministic
comparison identifies real changes before AI analysis begins. Validated changes
can then feed summaries, alerts, reports, AskGlow and evidence-linked battle
cards.

The surrounding system coordinates scheduled monitoring, browser work, queues,
notifications and operational recovery. Each layer has a clear responsibility,
so AI adds judgment after the system has established reliable evidence.
