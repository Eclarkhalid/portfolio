Shotsfolio explored how one source asset could be transformed into useful
content for different channels without losing the original voice.

The product is no longer active. It remains in this portfolio as part of my
product history, not as a product that is still being sold or developed.

The work reinforced an important product lesson: transformation quality depends
on clear source context, constrained outputs and reviewable intermediate steps,
not only a larger prompt.
