Shotsfolio explored how one source asset could be transformed into useful
content for many different channels and formats without losing the original
voice.

The product is no longer active. It remains in this portfolio as part of my
product history, not as a product that is still being sold or developed.

## What I learned

Shotsfolio worked technically and attracted real users, but it never found
customers willing to pay for it. That gap taught me the most important lesson
of the product: **technical quality and product success are separate
problems.** A well-built product still needs a painful enough problem, a clear
buyer and a reason to pay now, and those are questions to answer before
building, not after launch.

The engineering lesson still holds too: transformation quality depends on clear
source context, constrained outputs and reviewable intermediate steps, not only
a larger prompt.
