Ryterr is a research and publishing system I founded and built for people who
need useful articles without treating a one-shot AI draft as finished work.

## The product problem

Generating prose is only one part of publishing. A credible article also needs
current sources, claim review, a consistent voice, usable images, clean
structure and a reliable path into the website that owns it.

## The engineering approach

Ryterr uses a staged pipeline for topic analysis, research, outlining, drafting,
search structure, images, fact checking, quality review and persistence.
Separating the work into explicit stages makes failures visible and allows each
stage to carry its evidence forward instead of hiding everything inside one
prompt.

The finished post remains portable. Ryterr produces Markdown and sanitized HTML,
keeps research and review records, and supports scheduled delivery through
webhooks, a Content API, WordPress, Ghost and GitHub. Delivery attempts are
tracked so publishing failures can be retried instead of silently losing work.
